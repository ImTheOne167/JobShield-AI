About JobShield AI

A smart platform designed to help job seekers identify suspicious job opportunities and stay protected from recruitment scams.

Our Mission

Making online job searching safer and smarter.

Finding a job online has become easier than ever, but unfortunately, fake job advertisements and recruitment scams have also increased.

JobShield AI was created to help students, fresh graduates and job seekers identify potential warning signs before trusting a job opportunity.

Our goal is simple: help people make safer and more informed decisions during their job search.

Why JobShield AI?

Because every job opportunity deserves a closer look.

Detect Suspicious Signals

The system looks for warning signs such as suspicious keywords, free email services, missing websites and other unusual information.

Calculate Risk

Detected warning signs are used to calculate an overall risk score for the submitted job.

Explain the Result

Instead of simply showing a result, JobShield AI provides reasons explaining why an opportunity may require additional caution.

How JobShield AI Works

A simple process from job information to risk assessment.

1. Submit Job Information

The user enters details such as job title, company name, recruiter email, salary, website, location and job description.

2. Analyze the Information

JobShield AI examines the submitted information for potential fraud indicators and suspicious patterns.

3. Generate Risk Score

The detected indicators contribute to a risk score between 0 and 100.

4. Display the Result

The opportunity is categorized as Genuine, Suspicious or Fake based on the current risk assessment system.

Our Detection Approach

Transparent and explainable risk assessment.

The current version of JobShield AI uses a rule-based risk assessment approach. It checks submitted job information for predefined warning signs and suspicious keywords.

Each detected indicator contributes points to the overall risk score. The final score is then used to classify the job opportunity.

Future versions can incorporate machine learning and natural language processing techniques to improve automated fake-job detection.

Important Disclaimer

JobShield AI provides a technology-based risk assessment and educational guidance. A result should not be considered absolute proof that a job opportunity or recruiter is genuine or fraudulent. Users should independently verify important employment information before sharing personal information or making payments.
